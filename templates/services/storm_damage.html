STORM DAMAGE &
INSURANCE RESTORATION

HELPING HOMEOWNERS NAVIGATE THE RESTORATION PROCESS WITH CONFIDENCE

Experiencing storm damage can be overwhelming. Between property inspections, insurance paperwork, construction decisions, and unexpected questions, many homeowners aren't sure where to begin.

Surface Shield Restoration is committed to making the restoration process easier by providing professional documentation, clear communication, and high-quality restoration services while helping property owners understand what to expect throughout the project.

HOW IT WORKS

OUR RESTORATION PROCESS

01

Property Inspection

Every project begins with a comprehensive inspection of the affected property. We evaluate roofing systems, siding, gutters, flashing, skylights, ventilation, and other exterior components for visible storm-related damage and document our findings with photographs and detailed observations.

02

Damage Documentation

Accurate documentation plays an important role in the restoration process. Our team prepares detailed information describing observed damage, measurements, photographs, and the restoration work necessary to return the property to a safe and functional condition.

03

Insurance Coordination

When requested by the property owner, we can communicate with the insurance company regarding the proposed restoration scope, answer contractor-related questions, and provide supporting documentation for completed work.

If previously hidden damage related to the covered loss is discovered during construction, additional documentation may be submitted for the insurance company's review. Coverage decisions are made solely by the insurance carrier in accordance with the terms and conditions of the policy.

04

Building Code Considerations

Building codes evolve over time. If current building code requirements affect the restoration process, we document those requirements and provide supporting information for review where appropriate.

05

Professional Restoration

Once the project has been approved and scheduled, our experienced crews complete the restoration using premium materials, professional installation practices, and careful attention to detail. Every project concludes with a thorough cleanup and final walkthrough.

COMMON QUESTIONS

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Can I choose my own contractor?

In most cases, yes. Property owners generally have the right to choose the contractor they would like to perform the restoration work.

What if additional damage is discovered?

Some damage may not be visible until construction begins. If additional storm-related damage is uncovered, it can be documented and submitted to the insurance company for review.

Will you communicate with my insurance company?

Yes. With your authorization, we can communicate directly with the insurance company regarding contractor-related aspects of the restoration process and provide supporting documentation when appropriate.

Can I upgrade my roofing materials?

Many homeowners choose to upgrade roofing materials or other exterior products during the restoration process. We'll discuss available options and any additional costs before work begins.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 1, 2026

Surface Shield Systems LLC ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

When you use our website, contact forms, or AI chat assistant, we may collect:

  • Contact information: name, phone number, and email address
  • Property information: service address or property location
  • Insurance information: insurance company name, claim number, and related details you provide when requesting storm damage or restoration services
  • Service preferences: type of service requested, project description, and preferred contact method
  • Conversation history: messages exchanged with our AI chat assistant
  • Technical data: pages visited, referral source, UTM campaign parameters, and session cookies necessary for secure form submission

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected to:

  • Respond to your service inquiries and provide estimates
  • Schedule inspections, consultations, and project work
  • Coordinate with insurance companies on your behalf when authorized
  • Send appointment reminders and project updates
  • Improve our services and website experience
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

3. Cookies and Session Data

Our website uses session cookies and a CSRF security token required for secure form submission. These are technical necessities and do not track you across other websites. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

4. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, provide ongoing services, and comply with applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or loss. Our website uses HTTPS encryption for all data transmission.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Opt out of non-essential communications at any time

To exercise these rights, contact us at info@surfaceshieldsystems.com or call +1 (216) 280-1855.

7. Third-Party Services

Our website may use third-party services for security verification (Cloudflare Turnstile) and AI-powered chat responses. These services process only the minimum data necessary to function and are governed by their own privacy policies.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under 18 years of age.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of our website after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
